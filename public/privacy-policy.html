OFFICIAL PRIVACY DISCLOSURE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026
Application: Vanguard Geo / Kerala Emergency Navigation & Dispatch

⚠️ Important Government Disclaimer:
This application is an independent tactical emergency navigation and disaster response utility. It is NOT an official government entity, and is NOT officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Kerala State Disaster Management Authority (KSDMA) or any district administration. Official emergency hotlines provided (such as 1077, 112, 108) connect directly to standard public emergency telephone switches.

1. Data We Collect and Why

  • Precise Location (GPS): Used exclusively to calculate local Dijkstra turn-by-turn routing, compute nearest hospital distances, and detect proximity to designated flood and landslide hazard zones. Location processing is performed locally on your device and is not sold or broadcast to third parties.
  • Camera & Photographic Proof: When filing an emergency incident report, you may optionally attach a photograph of ground conditions. Photos are stored locally in your device's IndexedDB and synced to your designated organization dispatch backend only when online.
  • Voice Input / Dictation: Dictated incident descriptions are transcribed via your device's native speech recognition engine solely for text field population. No audio recordings are permanently stored on our servers.

2. Data Storage & Local-First Processing

This application operates under a local-first design. Mission coordinates, map tiles, hazard polygons, and offline contacts remain stored on your device in local storage and IndexedDB. In offline mode, no data leaves your physical device.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT track users across other apps, and we do NOT sell, lease, or monetize personal user data with data brokers or advertising networks.

4. Peer-to-Peer QR Code Sharing

Tactical mission sharing via QR codes is completed entirely screen-to-screen without routing through external servers or cellular internet.

5. Contact & Data Deletion Requests

To clear all local cached data, incident histories, and map packs, use the "Clear Cached Data" option directly inside the System Console tab of the application or clear your browser/app storage.
