🚨 Critical Bugs Identified

BUG-01: Runtime Crash on SOS Contact SMS & Phone Buttons

Location: src/App.jsx:4872, 4884

Clicking "SMS" or "Call" on an incident card throws ReferenceError: sendSosSms is not defined and ReferenceError: callSosContact is not defined, crashing the React UI.

BUG-02: Runtime Crash on "Abort Mission" Simulation

Location: src/App.jsx:5602

Clicking "Abort Mission" during responder simulation throws ReferenceError: stopSimulation is not defined.

BUG-03: Incident Submission Blocked in Offline Disaster Zones

Location: src/App.jsx:4632

The "File Incident Report" button is strictly disabled without on-device AI hazard verification. In offline zones without external CDN access for MobileNet, responders cannot submit emergency reports.
