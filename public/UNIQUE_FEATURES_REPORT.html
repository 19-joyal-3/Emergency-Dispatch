⚡ Executive Summary: Why This Platform Is Unique

Standard commercial navigation solutions (such as Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze) are built strictly on the assumption of continuous high-speed internet connectivity, centralized cloud servers, and passive vehicular traffic telemetry. In real-world catastrophic events—such as the 2018/2024 Kerala floods, the Chooralmala-Meppadi landslides, or cyclone-induced grid collapses—cellular towers, fiber backbones, and power stations fail simultaneously.

The Kerala Emergency Dispatch Platform was engineered from scratch as a resilient, mission-critical edge platform designed specifically to operate when all telecommunication infrastructure is severed. It introduces a suite of 12 groundbreaking features spanning hardware-level BLE radio telemetry, physical RSSI distance modeling, real-time road vector severance, spatial photographic threat interception, and air-gapped QR data handoffs that exist nowhere else in consumer navigation.

🚀 Comprehensive Catalog of 12 Unique Capabilities

Feature 01

Zero-Connectivity Bluetooth & Wi-Fi Mesh Hardware Radar

Industry First in Web GIS

While standard dispatch platforms fail completely when cellular towers collapse, this hub features an integrated Zero-Connectivity P2P Engine. It utilizes the Web Bluetooth API, BroadcastChannel, and local WebRTC DataChannels to establish an autonomous ad-hoc peer mesh across devices within physical vicinity.

// Log-Distance Path Loss Formula for Estimating Real-World Survivor Distance (Meters):
Distance = 10 ^ ((Measured Power P0 - Current RSSI dBm) / (10 * Path Loss Exponent n))
Physical Distance Ranging

Translates raw decibel milliwatts (RSSI) directly into physical meters (e.g. -50 dBm = 0.5m, -75 dBm = ~5m, -90 dBm = ~25m) without GPS triangulation.

Azimuth Radar Display

Tactical circular radar scope with animated phosphor sweep, range rings (10m, 25m, 50m, 100m), and interactive pings that can be plotted with 1 click.

Authentic Hardware Interfacing

Direct Web Bluetooth pairing scans real Bluetooth Low Energy beacons, mobile phones, and rescue transmitters in disaster rubble.

Feature 02

Spatial Proximity Threat Interception & Citizen Photo Evidence Push

Proprietary Life-Safety Feature

Traditional mapping applications allow drivers to unwittingly navigate directly into landslides, flash floods, or raging forest fires. This system features an active Spatial Collision Intercept Monitor:

Automated Screen Takeover

Whenever an active driver, civilian, or responder enters within the threat perimeter (e.g., 2.5 km of an active disaster), the screen instantly displays the .hazard-intercept-card takeover modal.

Ground Photographic Proof

Unlike generic text alerts, the intercept displays the exact high-resolution photographic evidence uploaded from the disaster epicenter, proving the road is impassable.

Multi-Sensory Siren & Haptics

Fires an audio frequency alert tone alongside native hardware vibration pulses ([200, 100, 200, 100, 400]) to alert drivers even in noisy cabin environments.

Immediate Bypass Route

Presents an automated 1-click evacuation corridor rerouting the driver safely away from the active hazard zone.

Feature 03

Interactive Road Segment Severance & Dynamic Vector Graph Rerouting

Instant Operational Control

In military and disaster command, dispatchers cannot wait for third-party map vendors to update road closure databases hours or days later.

1-Click Physical Road Cutting

Dispatchers or field officers simply double-click any road segment on the interactive vector map to toggle it as "Severed / Submerged".

Instant Graph Penalty Calculation

The client-side Dijkstra & A* pathfinding engine assigns infinite weight to severed road segments, immediately calculating bypass corridors for heavy rescue convoys.

Visual Hazard Overlays

Severed segments instantly render with vibrant diagonal danger stripes and flashing warning endpoints.

Feature 04

Air-Gapped High-Density QR Incident & Tactical Route Handoff

Zero RF Emission Transfer

When electronic warfare, lightning strikes, or RF jamming renders radio communications risky or unavailable, rescue teams can transfer comprehensive tactical situational data optically from screen to screen.

Full Parametric Compression

Serializes casualty counts, GPS coordinates, triage urgency, and full route waypoint vectors into high-density air-gapped QR codes.

Zero Pairing or Interop Overhead

Any responding team (NDRF, Police, Coast Guard, Fishermen Squads) can scan the QR code using any camera and instantly load coordinates onto their devices.
