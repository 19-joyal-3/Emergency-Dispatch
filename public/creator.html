About the Creator

Joyal Thomas Francis is the sole creator, systems architect, and lead developer of Resylix (formerly Vanguard Geo). Based in Kerala, India, Joyal conceived and engineered Resylix as an autonomous, tactical disaster response platform engineered to save lives in the "Golden Hour" when cell towers collapse, power grids fail, and fiber cables wash away during extreme monsoonal disasters.

Witnessing recurrent catastrophe corridors across Kerala—including the devastating Wayanad landslides (Chooralmala & Mundakkai), severe Kuttanad flooding, and Western Ghats mountain road collapses—Joyal engineered a 100% offline-first software architecture capable of providing uninterrupted GPS navigation and tactical resource coordination without requiring cellular data or an internet connection.

🎯 Mission & Purpose

"When disasters sever telecommunications, mainstream apps like Google Maps display 'No Internet Connection' and become unusable. Resylix was built so that emergency responders, local volunteers, and stranded citizens always have a deterministic, working road navigation engine in their pocket."

Key Technologies Engineered by Joyal Thomas Francis

  • Zero-Connectivity Topological Vector Routing: An in-browser Dijkstra and A* graph routing engine capable of calculating optimal bypasses across Kerala highways and rural hamlets completely offline.
  • 5.0 KM Threat Proximity Radar & Automatic Detour: Spatial early warning system that detects upcoming landslides, floodings, and road blockages within 5.0 km, recalculating safe bypass corridors automatically.
  • KSDMA Reservoir & Rule Curve Telemetry: Integration of 24 monitored Kerala reservoirs with Central Water Commission (CWC) Blue, Orange, and Red alert stages and downstream flood safety zones.
  • Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Hazard Mesh: Decentralized communication protocols using WebRTC and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to transmit roadblock coordinates directly between responder vehicles.
  • Bilingual Voice Guidance: Native spoken audio feedback in both English and Malayalam (മലയാളം) tailored for grassroots rescue workers and panchayat task forces.
