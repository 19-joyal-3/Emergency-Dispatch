Resylix (Vanguard Geo) | Offline Tactical Emergency Navigation & Hazard Mesh

Resylix (formerly Vanguard Geo) is an offline-first tactical emergency navigation, zero-connectivity peer-to-peer hazard mesh dispatch, and disaster response platform for Kerala.

Mission-Critical Tactical Features

  • Deterministic Offline Navigation: High-performance graph routing across Kerala highways, mountain ghat roads, and rural hamlets without cellular data.
  • 1-Click 5.0 KM Tactical Proximity Scan: Instant radar scan locating nearest trauma hospitals, 24/7 fuel stations, and relief shelters around any field location or incident scene.
  • 210 Verified Kerala Facilities Directory: Search and filter across all 14 districts with 24/7 emergency switches, contact numbers, and direct offline routing.
  • Field Road Blockage & Landslide Quick-Report: Places instant barriers for landslides, flash floods, or submerged bridges with automated 5km detour recalculation and spoken audio warnings.
  • KSDMA Weather Matrix & Dam Monitor: Live 14-district IMD alert tracking (Red/Orange/Yellow) and 24 major reservoir rule curve levels.
  • V2V Hazard Mesh & BLE Radar: Decentralized peer-to-peer relay distributing landslide, flood, and roadblock telemetry directly between responder vehicles and citizens.
  • Official Evacuation Manifest PDF: One-click generation of disaster clearance manifests with SEOC 1070 and DEOC 1077 hotlines.

All 14 Kerala Administrative Districts Covered

Full offline topological vector routing across Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, Kottayam, Idukki, Ernakulam, Thrissur, Palakkad, Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad (Meppadi, Chooralmala, Mundakkai), Kannur, and Kasaragod.
